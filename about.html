About Us

A trusted media company with deep roots in local communities.

Our Story

Edwards Group Holdings represents decades of commitment to community journalism and media excellence. Founded with the vision of connecting communities through trusted communication, we are a multi-state media company serving diverse markets across three states.

Our journey began with a simple belief: that local communities deserve quality journalism, engaging entertainment, and professional services that reflect their values and serve their needs.

Today, we operate newspapers, radio stations, and printing companies that have become integral parts of their communities. We take pride in being more than just media companies: we are community partners, local employers, and trusted voices.
